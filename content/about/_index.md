# About Project Koku

Koku is an open source cost management platform for clouds, containers, and
hybrid environments. It is the upstream project for
[Red Hat Lightspeed Cost Management](https://access.redhat.com/products/cost-management).

Koku identifies, reports, and analyzes cost data across a wide range of
infrastructure sources — enabling enterprises and sovereign cloud providers to
gain visibility into costs, optimize resource usage, and integrate financial
data with their billing, ERP, and business intelligence systems.

## The Name

**Koku** (石), in Japanese, is a unit of measure for the amount of rice
sufficient for one person for one year. **Masu** (枡) is a square wooden box
used for measuring rice — and more recently, for drinking sake.

Along the themes of measurement and management of resources, the Koku logo
depicts a masu box measuring the rice cloud.
